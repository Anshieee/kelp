Setup effort Hermes & co. You install it and set it up yourself. Expect a few technical steps, and some upkeep. Simple assistants Quick. A guided sign-up, then straight into a conversation. Kelp Our aim: none. You send a message and Kelp works on a computer of its own.
What it can do Hermes & co. Broad. You choose the models and tools, and shape how the agent works. Simple assistants Everyday help: questions, writing, simple reminders. Kelp Longer, multi-step work. Researching, drafting a document, following up on an invoice.
Limits Hermes & co. Freedom brings choices. Someone has to keep it running and up to date. Simple assistants A narrower scope. Fewer jobs that carry on without you. Kelp Still a preview. Kelp asks before anything that costs money or changes your files.
Who it suits Hermes & co. People who enjoy tinkering and want to own the whole setup. Simple assistants Anyone who wants a quick, low-effort start. Kelp People who want the work done without learning the plumbing.

This standalone landing page is a product preview, not the live Kelp service. Dashboard links use app.kelp.local, a placeholder domain. The service privacy policy, operator details, and contact information are not yet supplied.
